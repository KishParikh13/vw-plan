The money

Two clocks, running together

One brings money this quarter. The other builds donors for years to come.

Give Now — the warm circle

Current students, WhatsApp groups, the India email list, personal networks. Seed money, available today.

Move: a clear ask with a real goal ("our first $25,000"), plus a monthly option.

Give Later — the funnel

Clips and email turn strangers into people who trust us. Some become donors over months.

Move: give value free, then ask gently by email. Never ask a stranger cold.

Name what the money does. "Sponsor a student" beats "please donate."

Keep it free

Videos, weekly classes, and free talks stay open to all.

Sponsor a student

Cover a course or the year for someone who can't afford it.
