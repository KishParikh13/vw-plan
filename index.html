The big idea

Not "ancient scripture." A clear way to steady a busy, anxious mind.

We lead with the problems young people feel every day, stress, comparison, burnout, "what am I doing with my life," and let Vedanta's answer be the pleasant surprise. Dr. Janki's calm, logical teaching is the trust. Her face and voice come first, always. We don't make more content, we share what already exists, better.

Her own numbers already tell us what works. The videos that reached tens of thousands all name a real feeling, then answer it: "Why does work become stressful?" (7,000), "One habit that can bring you happiness" (4,600), "Beat stress with Vedanta" (4,200). Today's videos open with slides and text, and reach under 150. The recipe: name a feeling → answer it with Vedanta → put Dr. Janki's face first.

18 to 25 — the seekers

Where: TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts. Vedanta Wisdom has no TikTok at all today, the biggest gap.

What works: short punchy clips that name a real feeling in the first two seconds. They share what makes them feel understood.

35 to 45 — the ready ones

Where: LinkedIn, podcasts, word of mouth. They have the career and the family, and something still feels off.

What works: Dr. Janki's intellectual rigor and "applied philosophy." A podcast or a talk lands harder than a Reel. Faster to convert, larger gift size.

What this means for effort: short clips serve the 18-25 group and reach some 35-45s too. So we do clips first. Podcasts and LinkedIn come when we have a second volunteer.

Donations & courses

Where the money comes from, now and later

Two clocks run at the same time. One brings money this quarter. The other builds the donors of the next few years.

Give Now — the warm circle

Current students, the WhatsApp groups, the existing India email list, and personal networks. This is the seed money, and it is available today.

The move: a clear, repeated ask with a real goal ("raise our first $25,000 to launch the US chapter"), plus a monthly giving option. Warm asks, not cold.

Give Later — the content funnel

The short videos and email list turn strangers into people who trust us. A share become donors over months. Slower, but it grows the donor base beyond the founding circle.

The move: keep giving value freely, then ask gently by email once trust is built. Never ask a stranger cold.

Name what the money does. "Sponsor a student" beats "please donate."

Keep the teaching free

The videos, weekly classes, and free talks stay open to everyone. Your gift covers reaching more people.

Sponsor a student

Help someone attend the Foundation Course or the year-long program who could not otherwise afford it.

Build the University

The long vision: a residential Vedanta university. A bold, lasting cause for larger and legacy gifts.

Monthly sustainers

A named circle of monthly givers (like Isha's "Sanghamitra") is the steadiest money a nonprofit can have. Ask one-time givers to become monthly after their first gift.

Grants

Foundations and government grants can dwarf small gifts. This track is already in motion. It needs one person to own the research and applications.

Year-end push

Most US giving happens in December and on Giving Tuesday. Plan one focused seasonal appeal to the whole list then.
