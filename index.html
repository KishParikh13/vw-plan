Audience

Two groups, one plan

18 to 25 — the seekers

Feel: stress, comparison, "what am I doing with my life."

Reach on: TikTok, Reels, Shorts. We have no TikTok yet, the biggest miss.

Lands: short clips that name a real feeling in 2 seconds.

35 to 45 — the ready ones

Feel: have the career and family, something still off.

Reach on: LinkedIn, podcasts, word of mouth.

Lands: Dr. Janki's clear thinking. They give more, sooner.

Clips come first. They serve the young group and reach some older ones too. Podcasts and LinkedIn wait for a second helper.

Donations engine

Two clocks, running together

One brings money this quarter. The other builds donors for years.

Live today

vedantawisdom.us/donate

Registered 501(c)(3). Gifts are tax-deductible. Runs on Zeffy with zero fees, so $100 arrives as $100.

Give Now — the warm circle

Current students, WhatsApp groups, the India list, personal networks. Seed money, today.

Move: a clear ask with a real goal ("our first $25,000"), plus a monthly option.

Give Later — the funnel

Clips and email turn strangers into people who trust us. Some become donors over months.

Move: give value free, then ask gently by email. Never ask a stranger cold.

Name what the money does. "Sponsor a student" beats "please donate."

Keep it free

Videos, classes, and free talks stay open to all.

Sponsor a student

Cover a course or the year for someone who can't afford it.

Build the University

The long vision: a residential Vedanta university. For legacy gifts.

Monthly givers

The steadiest money there is. Ask one-time givers to go monthly.
